> “All I want to know is where I’m going to die, so I’ll never go there.” — Charlie Munger

> “People think focus means saying yes to the thing you’ve got to focus on. But that’s not what it means at all. It means saying no to the hundred other good ideas that there are. You have to pick carefully. I’m actually as proud of the things we haven’t done as the things I have done. Innovation is saying no to 1,000 things.” — Steve Jobs

下面是我還在練習的拒做清單（Anti-To-Do List）：

* 不要急 [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]
* 不要追 [^5]
* 不要抱怨 [^6]
* 不要擔心 [^7] [^8] [^9]
* 不要屈就平庸
* 不要預設立場
* 不要和別人比較 [^10]
* 不要對自己失望 [^11]
* 不要過度苛責自己 [^12]
* 不要嘗試討好任何人 [^13]
* 不要嘗試說服任何人
* 不要嘗試改變任何人
* 不要對做自己感到抱歉 [^20] [^14]
* 不要想超出控制範圍的事
* 不要給別人沒有問的建議
* [不要浪費時間](https://huam.ing/death-clock)，一秒都不行。[^15]
* 不要跟別人說你還沒辦到的事 [^16] [^17]
* [不要太相信自己相信的事](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/95742516-don-t-believe-everything-you-think-why-your-thinking-is-the-beginning)，保持初學者心態，擁抱無知。[^18]
* 不要追逐曇花一現的身外之物，專注在 [勞動](https://huam.ing/2026-10-30) 才能換來的長久收穫。[^19]
