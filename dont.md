> 我只想知道去哪邊會掛掉，然後遠離那些地方。
> — 查理蒙格

下面是我還在練習的拒做清單（Anti-To-Do List）：

* 不要急 [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]
* 不要追 [^5]
* 不要抱怨 [^6]
* 不要擔心 [^7] [^8] [^9]
* 不要屈就平庸
* 不要和別人比較 [^10]
* 不要對自己失望 [^11]
* 不要嘗試說服任何人
* 不要嘗試改變任何人
* 不要給別人沒有問的建議
* [不要浪費時間](https://huam.ing/death-clock)，一秒都不行。
* [不要太相信自己相信的事](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/95742516-don-t-believe-everything-you-think-why-your-thinking-is-the-beginning)，保持初學者心態，擁抱無知。[^12]
* 不要追逐曇花一現的身外之物，專注在 [勞動](https://huam.ing/2026-10-30) 才能換來的長久收穫。[^13]

[^1]: 例如：[趕公車時，堅持不跑起來](https://noa.bearblog.dev/a-little-bit-of-spine/)。
[^2]: _“Relax. Nothing is under control.” — Adi Da_
[^3]: _“Slow down, you’re doing fine. You can’t be everything you want to be before your time.” — Billy Joel_
[^4]: _“Rivers know this: there is no hurry. We shall get there some day.” — A.A. Milne, [Winnie-the-Pooh](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/1225592)_
[^5]: _“If you spend your time chasing butterflies, they’ll fly away. But if you spend your time mending a beautiful garden, the butterflies will come.” — Mario Quintana_
[^6]: _“If you don’t like something, change it. If you can’t change it, change your attitude. Don’t complain.” — Maya Angelou_
[^7]: _“There is only one way to happiness and that is to cease worrying about things which are beyond the power or our will.” — Epictetus_
[^8]: _“Little minds have little worries, big minds have no time for worries.” — Ralph Waldo Emerson_
[^9]: _“A good question to ask yourself today is ‘What might I be wrong about?’ This is the only worry worth having.” — Kevin Kelly, [Excellent Advice for Living: Wisdom I Wish I’d Known Earlier](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/98113859)_
[^10]: Comparison is the thief of joy.
[^11]: _“Your job, throughout your entire life, is to disappoint as many people as it takes to avoid disappointing yourself.” — Glennon Doyle, [Untamed](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/73600042)_
[^12]: _“Because a huge percentage of the stuff that I tend to be automatically certain of is, it turns out, totally wrong and deluded.” — David Foster Wallace_
[^13]: 例如：健康的身體、平靜的內心、充滿愛的家。
