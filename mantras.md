description: What I keep telling myself
modified: 2026-11-18

* Do what you can’t.
* No hurry. No pause.
* Be sincere, not serious.
* Human being, not doing.
* I am him inside and out, always here and now.
* Work hard in silence. Let your work make the noise.
* Live simple, work hard, be honest, then you are free.
* Work hard until you no longer have to introduce yourself.
* Remain as calm as the river’s surface, yet as strong as its current.
* I will not be another flower, picked for my beauty, and left to die. I will be wild, difficult to find, and impossible to forget.
